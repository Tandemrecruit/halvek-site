Why not do it in-house, or send it somewhere else?

  • Tested before it ships. Fit is proven in plastic before your part is final: against your sample, or with a fit coupon you try on your own hardware.
  • One price, set up front. The price you agree to is the price you pay, plus shipping at cost and any sales tax. Anything outside the package is a change order you approve first.
  • Files you can change. Fitment and product design jobs come with STEP and 3MF files and a print profile, so you can print the part again or edit it in your own CAD. Every design is built parametric, so a version for another machine is a quick change order instead of a new design.
  • Rights in writing. A permanent license to make and use fitment parts, and outright ownership of product designs, agreed before work starts.

  • Doing it in-house

    Someone on your team spends the hours: CAD, measuring, and reprinting until it fits. Worth it if you have the skill and the time to spare.

  • A freelance CAD designer

    Often cheaper per hour, but the job usually ends with a file. Finding out how it prints, and fixing what doesn't fit, is left to you.

  • An online print service

    Cheap and fast per part, but it prints exactly the file you send. Someone still has to design the part and make it fit.

  • Halvek

    Design, fit checks in plastic, and the files or the finished part for one set price, with local drop-off around Portage and Kalamazoo.

Common questions

Anything else, ask by email. You'll get a straight answer.

Do I need a 3D printer?
No. Every package includes printed parts shipped to you. Fitment and product design packages also include the files and a print profile; for replacement parts, the files are optional.
Do I own the design?
Product designs, yes: they come with a written IP assignment, so the design is yours outright. Fitment parts come with STEP and 3MF files and a permanent license to make and use the part. Halvek keeps the design, including the Fusion source file, and can offer it to others; to keep it yours alone, with the Fusion file, buy it outright for about 25% more. With a replacement part, you own the part, and Halvek keeps the model on file so another one is just a reprint. If you'd rather print your own, the files are yours outright for about 25% more.
Can I sell prints of it on Etsy?
Etsy requires 3D-printed items to come from the seller's own original design, and it hasn't said how commissioned designs count. A product design you own is yours on paper, but how Etsy treats it is Etsy's call, so check before you list.
Is there anything you won't design?
Parts where a failure could hurt someone: brakes, steering, suspension and other parts that control a vehicle; aircraft parts; structural supports, lifting gear and overhead mounts for anything heavy enough to hurt someone if it fell; fuel, gas and pressure parts; parts that enclose or insulate line-voltage wiring; machine guards and safety gear; anything that touches food; children's products; and medical devices. Also copies of another designer's files, and parts carrying logos or trademarks you don't own.
When do I pay?
Fitment parts and product design: half with the signed quote, and the rest once you've approved the fit or prototype, before the finished parts and files ship. Replacement parts: in full when you accept the firm quote, after your sample arrives. If the part doesn't fit, the first revision and reprint are free.
Can I drop a part off locally?
Yes, in the Portage and Kalamazoo area, by arrangement. Everything else ships.
